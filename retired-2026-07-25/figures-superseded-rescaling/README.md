# Withdrawn 4 August 2026: figures drawn against the pre-correction rescaling

`FIG_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.png` draws the comparison as 50 percent expected against
34.1 percent measured. Both figures came from rescaling the measured
heterozygous current to a two-allele heart by dividing by two, which assumes the
two copies add perfectly.

O'Neill 2022 measured the two-allele case directly: WT+WT cells read 218.4 plus
or minus 7.7 percent of a single wild-type allele (n = 199), not 200. Dividing
by 2.184 gives 31.3 percent measured against a 45.8 percent comparator. The
correction is recorded in the manuscript
`PUBLISH_2_BASE_EDITOR_SPECIFICITY.md`, section "A correction to the rescue
baseline, made 4 August 2026".

The figure is kept here rather than deleted so the correction can be seen. Its
third panel, the sample size required against assumed cell-to-cell variability,
was computed against the old pair. The gap it was sizing was 15.9 points and is
now 14.5, so that panel needs recalculating and not merely relabelling.
