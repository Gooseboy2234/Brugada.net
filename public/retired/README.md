# Retired documents, withdrawn from the public site

These seven files were served at `brugada.net/downloads/` until the 2026-08 rebuild. They are kept here
as a record of what was published, and they are no longer served.

They were withdrawn because the project's own document audit of 2026-07-25
(`SCN5A_R104Q_EVERYTHING_20260711/_DEPRECATED/README.md`) placed the work they describe in
`superseded_science/`, which that audit defines as "documents making scientific claims that are now
known to be wrong, or built on a premise that has since been retired. Do not cite these."

## Why each was withdrawn

| File | Reason |
|---|---|
| `manuscript-r104q-v1.md` | "Every load-bearing element of the thesis is retired": the title claim, the isolated-domain simulations it rests on, and the pharmacochaperone arm. |
| `canonical-facts.md` | Declared itself the single source of truth all other documents must cite. That role passed to `PROJECT_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.json`. The audit also found it self-contradictory. |
| `open-letter-to-a-bench-lab.md` | Solicits laboratories to run a co-expression experiment to determine whether the variant is dominant-negative. That status is measured and published, so the ask was already answered. |
| `wetlab-assay-package.md` | Same defect: its gating experiment is answered, and the rescue panel behind it depends on the demoted route. |
| `master-scoreboard.md` | Reports the compound screen as the current result. The screen belongs to the retired campaign. |
| `dossier-zinc000004286767.md` | Dossier for the screen's confirmed hit. Retired with the campaign. |
| `dossier-zinc000016526277.md` | Dossier for the screen's conditional hit. Retired with the campaign. |

## What replaced the underlying claim

The pharmacochaperone route rested on folding-stability prediction identifying which variants break the
channel. Tested against four variants already known to break it, that method identified one. The result
is published as a negative result rather than buried, and the route is listed as closed on the rebuilt
site's routes page with that measurement named as the blocker.

The route also carries a hazard that is independent of the calibration failure: if the broken copy
interferes with the healthy one, then helping more broken protein reach the cell surface could worsen
rather than improve the outcome.

## Nothing here was deleted

These files are the versions as published. They are retained so that anyone who read or cited the
earlier site can see exactly what was withdrawn and why. They should not be cited as current.
