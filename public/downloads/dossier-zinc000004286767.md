## 4. Convergence status

**Formally converged? No** (`all_convergence_ok = false`). This is stated plainly because a reviewer flags it first.

- **Within-replica precision: excellent at 5 ns** — tight CIs, plateau SDs 0.19–1.45 kcal/mol, from 2.5× longer per-window sampling than the 2 ns wave-1.
- **Between-replica spread: SD 4.38 kcal/mol** — the three replicas settle into genuinely different well depths (−13 to −22 kcal/mol).
- **Interpretation (from the verdict artifact):** the clip well is deep and **sign-consistent across all replicas** (every replica strongly negative). Longer per-window sampling dramatically tightened each replica's internal precision but did **not** collapse the between-replica spread — this is a **real basin-sampling effect** (the bound complex has multiple sub-states of differing depth), not a simple convergence artifact. The magnitude therefore carries a genuine ±4 kcal/mol replicate uncertainty even at gold-standard per-window sampling.
- **Board-wide root cause:** 5 ns/window at 0.5 Å spacing gives only ~2–3% adjacent-window phase-space overlap (want 10–30%), so MBAR stitches every profile from poorly-connected windows.
- **Fix in progress:** REUS (replica-exchange umbrella sampling) on the owned z840 — replicas swap across windows so phase space connects and the profile converges. **REUS-converged ΔG is pending, not estimated.**
